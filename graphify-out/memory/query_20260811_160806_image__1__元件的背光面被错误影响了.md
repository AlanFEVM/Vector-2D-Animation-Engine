# Q: [Image #1] 元件的背光面被错误影响了

## Answer

Expanded from original query via graph vocab: [light, point, surface, plane, normal, view, direction, receiver, camera, local, render, optical]. TryGetReference3DSurfaceSample defines viewDirection as surface-to-camera. The earlier per-light plane flip made every finite light front-facing and allowed backside leakage. The corrected finite-light plane is oriented once toward the camera-visible side for zero-thickness FrontFill/FrontStroke receivers; point and area samples on the opposite side are then rejected, while extruded surfaces keep their authored one-sided planes.

## Outcome

- Signal: corrected
- Correction: Orient a zero-thickness finite-light receiver plane toward viewDirection once, then reject point/area samples with non-positive signed distance; never orient the receiver separately toward each light.
